Installed — next step: configure

The LOTUS Child is installed at /Applications/LOTUS Child/.

  1. Open Applications → LOTUS Child → Configure LOTUS Child.
  2. Paste the mother's IP and shared token when prompted.
  3. Open Start LOTUS Child to launch the worker. It'll auto-announce on your local network.

The mother (on your main Mac) should show a join alert in its dashboard within a few seconds, and this machine's agents will start appearing in the mother's Agent Mesh strip.
