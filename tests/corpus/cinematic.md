# FILM — THE LAST FOUNDRY

## TREATMENT

A retired metallurgist returns to the shuttered foundry where she spent
thirty years, on the night before it's demolished. 90 seconds, no dialogue.

## STYLE BIBLE

Anamorphic 2.39:1, Kodak Vision3 500T pushed one stop, heavy halation on
practical sodium lamps. Palette is rust orange against cold blue-grey
shadow. Handheld, long lenses, shallow focus. Grain visible in the blacks.

## ASSET 1 — ELENA VOSS

### NB2 PROMPT

```
Full-body character reference sheet, neutral A-pose, plain seamless grey
backdrop, flat even studio lighting, no shadows. Woman, 68, Eastern
European, 5'4", stocky build. Deeply lined face, heavy brow, grey hair cut
short and practical. Wearing a faded navy work coat over a grey wool
sweater, canvas trousers, steel-toed boots worn through at the left toe.
Burn scar across the back of the right hand, four inches, silvered with
age. Reading glasses hanging on a cord. Expression closed, watchful.
Full figure visible head to feet, sharp focus throughout.
```

## ASSET 2 — THE FOUNDRY FLOOR

### NB2 PROMPT

```
Establishing plate, no people present. Interior of an abandoned steel
foundry at night. Cathedral-scale space, riveted iron columns, a dormant
blast furnace at the far end. Sodium vapour lamps still burning in two
places, everything else in darkness. Standing water on the concrete floor
reflecting the lamps. Dust suspended in the air. Chains hanging from an
overhead crane gantry. Rust orange against cold blue-grey. Anamorphic
wide, deep perspective down the length of the hall.
```

## ACT 1 — ARRIVAL

### SHOT

Extreme wide, 40mm anamorphic, low and static. She enters frame left.

### NB2 PROMPT

```
Wide interior of the abandoned foundry at night — cathedral-scale hall,
riveted iron columns, dormant blast furnace far end, two sodium lamps
burning, standing water reflecting them, dust in the air. Elena Voss, 68,
stocky, short grey hair, faded navy work coat over grey sweater, canvas
trousers, worn steel-toed boots, stands small in the lower left third,
back to camera, facing the length of the hall. Anamorphic 2.39:1, Kodak
500T, heavy halation on the lamps, rust orange against blue-grey, grain in
the blacks, shallow focus on her, hall falling soft behind.
```

### ACT PROMPT

```
She takes three slow steps forward into the hall, boots breaking the
standing water. Camera holds static. Dust drifts through the lamp beams.
Ends with her stopped, still small in frame.
```

## ACT 2 — THE FURNACE

### SHOT

Medium close, 100mm, handheld, slow push.

### NB2 PROMPT

```
Medium close-up of Elena Voss, 68, deeply lined face, heavy brow, short
grey hair, reading glasses on a cord at her chest, faded navy work coat.
She faces the dormant blast furnace, lit from below by a single sodium
lamp, one side of her face in warm orange, the other in cold blue-grey
shadow. Burn scar visible on the back of her right hand, raised toward the
furnace door. Anamorphic 2.39:1, Kodak 500T pushed, heavy halation,
shallow focus, grain in the blacks.
```

### ACT PROMPT

```
Slow handheld push in on her face as she raises her scarred hand toward
the furnace door. Her eyes close. The sodium lamp flickers once. Ends
tight on her closed eyes.
```

## SOUND

Room tone only until Act 2, then a single low furnace rumble that isn't
there.
